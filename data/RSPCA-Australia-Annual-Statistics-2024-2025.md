The RSPCA receives thousands of animals every
year in every state and territory in Australia. Annual
statistics on the numbers of animals received,
reclaimed or rehomed are compiled on a national
basis by RSPCA Australia. The following statistics
cover the 2024 to 2025 financial year and give
details on the receipt and placement of animals by
each state and territory RSPCA during that period.
The statistics also include the number of cruelty
complaints received by the RSPCA Inspectorate and
prosecutions undertaken.
